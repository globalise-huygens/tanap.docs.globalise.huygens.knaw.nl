# Source files for https://docs.globalise.huygens.knaw.nl/tanap/

- The folder [TANAP inventories and Transcriptions received from National Archives 20250117](https://github.com/globalise-huygens/tanap.docs.globalise.huygens.knaw.nl/tree/source-files/TANAP%20inventories%20and%20Transcriptions%20received%20from%20National%20Archives%2020250117) contains files received by the GLOBALISE project from the National Archives of the Netherlands in January 2025.
  - `TANAP Transcriptions.zip`: a collection of XML-TEI files containing manually transcribed documents from the Orphan Chamber and Council of Policy of the VOC settlement at the Cape of Good Hope.
  - `TANAP inventories.zip`: a collection of inventories of VOC-related archives worldwide, which used to be available on the TANAP website (now offline).
- `tanap-website.zip`: the Web Archive mirror image with timestamp 20180627203307 of the former TANAP website.
- `tei-to-md.ipynb`: Jupyter notebook for converting the XML-TEI files of the Cape of Good Hope transcriptions and related resources into Markdown format.
