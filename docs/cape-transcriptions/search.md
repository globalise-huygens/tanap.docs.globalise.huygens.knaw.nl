# Search the Cape of Good Hope transcriptions

Search the full text of the [Cape of Good Hope transcriptions](index.md), one collection at a time. Use the filters to narrow the results down to specific volumes.

Search tips:

- Words are matched from the start, so `Valcken` also finds *Valckenrijck*. Spelling in the transcriptions is not standardised, so try variants (*schip*, *schipp*, *scheep*).
- Put words in quotes to find an exact phrase: `"Raad van Justitie"`.
- Dates are written as `{YYYYMMDD}` in the transcriptions, so searching `167002` finds dates in February 1670.
